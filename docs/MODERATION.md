# FunBytes Content Moderation & Safety

## 1. User Reporting Workflow
Users can flag any post or comment through the `⋯ More` menu:
- **Misinformation**
- **Hate Speech / Harassment**
- **Spam / Commercial Promotion**
- **Copyright Infringement**
- **Inappropriate Content**

## 2. Automated Safety & Soft-Hiding
- Any item receiving $\ge 3$ unique reports is automatically flagged for moderator review.
- High-severity keyword filters hide toxic comments immediately before review.
- Source-level kill switch allows administrators to instantly pause ingestion from compromised or low-quality feeds.
